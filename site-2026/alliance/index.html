The alliance

Different strengths. One regional voice.

Coimbatore NXT is powered by leading industry, trade, built-environment, environmental and civic organisations working beyond institutional boundaries on priorities no single organisation can solve alone.

Collaboration is our operating advantage.

Meet the partners Work with us
Why the alliance matters

Regional challenges do not follow institutional boundaries.

Infrastructure, investment, employment, planning, sustainability and brand-building affect the entire region. The alliance enables member organisations to combine specialised knowledge and trusted networks while retaining their own identities and mandates.

Partner organisations

The institutions behind Coimbatore NXT.

Each partner contributes a distinct constituency and capability—from industry and commerce to planning, environment, civic action and youth leadership.

  • CIIConfederation of Indian IndustryIndustry and business networkContributes industry perspective, national networks and sectoral expertise to the regional agenda.
  • CODISSIACODISSIAMSME and industrial ecosystemConnects the region’s small and medium industrial base with shared growth and infrastructure priorities.
  • ICCICIndian Chamber of Commerce and Industry, CoimbatoreCommerce and regional enterpriseProvides a broad commercial platform and convening capability across regional business interests.
  • KGFKongu Global ForumRegional connectivity and advocacyContributes research, advocacy and stakeholder engagement across critical regional infrastructure priorities.
  • CREDAICREDAI CoimbatoreBuilt environment and urban developmentBrings development, planning and built-environment expertise to the city-region agenda.
  • SIEMASouthern India Engineering Manufacturers’ AssociationEngineering manufacturingRepresents engineering and manufacturing capability and supports investment-enablement workstreams.
  • SIMASouthern India Mills’ AssociationTextilesBrings sector leadership to the transformation, competitiveness and sustainability of the textile ecosystem.
  • IIFIndian Institute of FoundrymenFoundry and manufacturingContributes specialist industrial knowledge from a sector central to the region’s engineering economy.
  • SiruthuliSiruthuliWater and environmentBrings civic experience and domain commitment to water security and environmental resilience.
  • RAACResidents Awareness Association of CoimbatoreCivic systems and liveabilityContributes citizen perspective and practical engagement on civic and urban-system priorities.
  • YiYoung IndiansYouth leadershipConnects young leaders and future-facing perspectives to the region’s collective agenda.

Partner with us

A clear role

Coimbatore NXT works where collective action adds value.

Association-specific services, member programmes and statutory decisions remain with the relevant organisations. Coimbatore NXT focuses on shared regional priorities, cross-sector initiatives and coordinated external engagement.
