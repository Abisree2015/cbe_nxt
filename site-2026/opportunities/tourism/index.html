Explore Coimbatore and the wider region.

Coimbatore is both a destination and a gateway: a working city with its own industrial heritage, food, temples and contemporary culture, connected to the Western Ghats and many of South India’s most distinctive landscapes.

Plan your regional discovery

Build your itinerary around

  • Local heritage, markets, food, craft and industrial stories.
  • Temples, spiritual centres and wellness experiences.
  • The Nilgiris, hill towns and scenic routes in the wider region.
  • Nature, wildlife and responsible outdoor experiences.
  • Business events, exhibitions, education and medical travel.

How Coimbatore NXT can help

We help strengthen the region’s identity and visibility. Travel conditions, access permissions, operating hours and bookings should be verified with official tourism bodies and service providers.
