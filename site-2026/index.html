Coimbatore NXT

One Region. One Voice. One Shared Future.

Coimbatore NXT brings institutions, industry and civic leaders together to turn regional ambition into coordinated action—and position the wider Coimbatore region among Asia’s fastest-growing, most liveable Tier-2 economies.

The city where anything is possible.

Built by enterprise

Coimbatore has always known how to make things happen.

Across engineering, textiles, technology, education, healthcare and social enterprise, the region has repeatedly converted constraint into capability. Coimbatore NXT exists to apply that same enterprising instinct to the region’s next chapter.

The opportunity now is not simply to grow. It is to grow with direction: creating better jobs, stronger infrastructure, globally competitive businesses, resilient natural systems and a city-region that remains deeply liveable.

The 2030 ambition

Towards a $100 billion regional economy.

This is an ambition, not a slogan. It requires stronger enterprises, new investment, high-value employment, future-ready skills, enabling infrastructure, innovation capacity, environmental resilience and a brand equal to the region’s capabilities.

Explore Vision 2030

Our operating focus

Growth. Visibility. Advocacy.

Three complementary pillars turn a broad regional aspiration into a practical programme of action.

How we work

  1. 01

    Growth

    Scale established strengths and build new engines of economic opportunity.

    Growth work helps local enterprises become more competitive, attracts responsible investment, enables sunrise sectors and expands high-value employment across the region.

  2. 02

    Visibility

    Make the region’s capabilities, achievements and opportunities easier to discover.

    Visibility work strengthens Coimbatore’s external narrative, communicates credible proof and connects the region with investors, talent, institutions, media and global networks.

  3. 03

    Advocacy

    Advance the infrastructure, policy and institutional priorities that no single organisation can secure alone.

    Advocacy work combines evidence, stakeholder alignment and sustained representation to improve connectivity, urban systems, environmental resilience and the conditions for enterprise.
