Vision 2030

A more prosperous, connected and liveable Coimbatore region.

Our 2030 ambition is to help build a $100 billion regional economy that creates high-value opportunity, strengthens global competitiveness and improves quality of life without compromising sustainability or inclusion.

Our north star

Prosperity that compounds across the region.

The $100 billion ambition provides a shared direction for 2030. The deeper objective is a diversified and resilient economy in which local firms scale, new sectors take root, high-value employment expands and public systems keep pace with growth.

Economic expansion is only durable when talent, infrastructure, inclusion, environmental health and quality of life advance with it.

Seven connected goals

A whole-region agenda.

The seven Charter goals are designed to reinforce one another. Infrastructure unlocks investment; skills and innovation raise productivity; sustainability protects long-term liveability; visibility brings opportunity; and high-value employment turns growth into shared prosperity.

See how delivery is organised

Growth

Scale established strengths and build new engines of economic opportunity.

4 goals

  1. 01

    Economic Growth

    Strengthen existing industries, attract investment and create new engines of regional value.

    Support productivity, scale, exports, technology adoption, market access and the emergence of sectors capable of creating sustained regional value.

  2. 02

    High-Value Employment Creation

    Expand resilient careers that reward skill, knowledge and innovation.

    Connect investment, enterprise growth and future sectors to better employment pathways while helping the region attract and retain talent.

  3. 04

    Skill Development

    Build future-ready talent through deeper industry–academia collaboration.

    Align learning, applied research and workforce development with the capabilities demanded by established and emerging sectors.

  4. 05

    Innovation and Entrepreneurship

    Turn engineering capability, research and enterprise into scalable innovation.

    Strengthen research, design, incubation, technology commercialisation and startup pathways across the regional ecosystem.

Visibility

Make the region’s capabilities, achievements and opportunities easier to discover.

1 goal

  1. 07

    Brand Building

    Build a clear, credible and globally relevant identity for the Coimbatore region.

    Present a unified regional narrative backed by proof, people and opportunities rather than generic promotion.

Advocacy

Advance the infrastructure, policy and institutional priorities that no single organisation can secure alone.

2 goals

  1. 03

    Infrastructure Development

    Secure the transport, logistics, urban and digital systems required for the next phase of growth.

    Advance evidence-led priorities across aviation, railways, roads, multimodal logistics, public transport, planning and enabling infrastructure.

  2. 06

    Sustainability

    Protect the natural and civic systems on which long-term prosperity depends.

    Advance water resilience, environmental stewardship, circularity, waste management and more sustainable patterns of urban and industrial growth.

What success looks like

A region that is easier to build in—and better to live in.

Explore the workstreams

  1. 01

    Existing enterprises scale into stronger national and global positions.

  2. 02

    New investment and sunrise sectors create resilient, high-value employment.

  3. 03

    Transport, logistics, urban planning and digital systems support the next phase of growth.

  4. 04

    Industry and academia jointly build future-ready talent and applied research capability.

  5. 05

    Water, waste and environmental systems become sources of resilience rather than constraints.

  6. 06

    Coimbatore earns visibility proportionate to its economic, intellectual and civic strengths.
