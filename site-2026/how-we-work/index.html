Three pillars

A simple model for a complex agenda.

  1. 01

    Growth

    Growth strengthens established industries and builds new engines of opportunity.

  2. 02

    Visibility

    Visibility presents the region’s real capabilities to investors, talent, policymakers and the wider world.

  3. 03

    Advocacy

    Advocacy advances the infrastructure, policy and institutional priorities required for long-term competitiveness and liveability.

The task-force cycle

Define. Evidence. Convene. Advocate. Deliver. Report.

View task forces

  1. 01

    Define

    Agree the problem, scope, outcome and ownership.

  2. 02

    Evidence

    Establish baselines, gather data and identify practical solutions.

  3. 03

    Convene

    Bring together the institutions, experts, users and authorities needed to move the issue.

  4. 04

    Advocate

    Make clear, coordinated and appropriately targeted representations.

  5. 05

    Deliver

    Execute actions within the task force’s mandate and support external decisions through follow-through.

  6. 06

    Report

    Track commitments, outcomes, blockers and escalation needs.

Accountability

Progress is managed, not merely discussed.

Each task force has a strategic mentor, a delivery lead and a core coordinator. Weekly working updates maintain momentum; monthly reports surface progress and blockers; quarterly reviews examine outcomes; and half-yearly reviews connect workstream impact to the 2030 agenda.

A Green–Amber–Red status system makes slippage and dependencies visible. Red items are escalated for the authority, access or decision required to unblock them.
